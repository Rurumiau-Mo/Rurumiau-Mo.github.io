Talk To Me

A Foundry VTT module designed to make your worlds feel just that little bit more alive.

Welcome to the Development Journal

Hello, and welcome!

I'm incredibly excited to finally share Talk To Me, a Foundry VTT module designed to make your worlds feel just that little bit more alive.

As a Game Master, I've always loved filling my worlds with bustling towns, wandering travellers, and memorable NPCs. The problem was that so many of those characters remained silent unless a player chose to interact with them. I wanted villages to feel busy, taverns to sound lively, and guards to mutter to one another as adventurers passed by.

That's where Talk To Me began.

The goal of the module is simple: give NPCs a voice.

✨ Current Features

At the moment, Talk To Me allows you to:

  • 💬 Display immersive speech bubbles above NPCs.
  • 📜 Pull dialogue directly from Foundry Roll Tables, making conversations easy to expand and randomise.
  • 🎭 Create interactive trigger tiles that can automatically display dialogue when activated.
  • 🧙 Choose any token on the scene to act as the speaker.
  • 🎥 Optionally pan and zoom the camera to the speaking character for cinematic moments.
  • ⚡ Generate helpful macros to make setting up conversations even faster.
  • 🧰 Multi-Use Tiles and Tile Chaining, including:
    • Lighting Tiles (Ambient and Global)
    • Teleport Tiles (with an optional Spawn Return)
    • Movement Tiles (with a Pathing Ruler)
    • Speech Tiles (with optional conversation chains)
    • Trap Tiles (in progress)

Whether you're building a crowded marketplace, a mysterious haunted manor, or simply want a guard to greet your players as they enter town, Talk To Me aims to make those moments effortless.

📦 Requirements

Talk To Me is built for Foundry Virtual Tabletop Version 14.

It also requires:

  • Monk's Active Tile Triggers (MATT)

This module handles the trigger system that allows dialogue to appear automatically as your players explore the world.
