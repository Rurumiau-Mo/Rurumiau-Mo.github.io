The witch behind the grimoire

Welcome Traveller~

Should you happen upon this book, know that its keeper answers to Ruru.

They possess an unusual fondness for enchanted forests, overgrown ruins, curious frogs, and one exceptionally fluffy Maine Coon named Mochi, who firmly believes every project requires feline supervision.

Their days are spent weaving stories, building adventures, and filling virtual worlds with little moments of magic. They believe every village deserves chatter, every forest deserves mystery, and every journey deserves something unexpected just beyond the trees.

If the birds are singing, the frogs are croaking, and the wind carries whispers through the branches, you'll probably find them happily at work nearby.

Should you choose to stay awhile, they would be delighted to have you. Just mind the mushrooms... some of them have opinions~

Made beneath mossy branches, under the sun and moon.
