The Moonlit Herbarium

An illustrated worldbuilding archive of fictional plants, their habitats, uses, myths, and magical properties.

❀

About the project

The Moonlit Herbarium began as a way to make fantasy environments feel more specific. Each plant is designed around the place it grows, the people who use it, and the stories attached to it.

This page is structured as a reusable project case study. Replace the title, cover, text, gallery items, and sidebar details with any project you want to showcase.

The idea

Every entry combines an illustration, a field note, a short piece of folklore, practical uses, and one unreliable rumour.

The visual direction

Rounded shapes, velvety greens, faded pinks, handwritten annotations, pressed flowers, and a softly magical natural-history-book atmosphere.
