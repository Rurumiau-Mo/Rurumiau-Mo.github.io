Foundry VTT Module

Talk To Me

A module concept for ambient NPC dialogue, chatter bubbles and reactive conversations inside Foundry VTT.

🐈‍⬛

Ambient Speech

NPCs can speak through on-screen bubbles, keeping chatter visible in the scene instead of cluttering the chat log.

Roll Table Support

Dialogue can be drawn from roll tables, making repeated interactions varied and easy for Game Masters to maintain.

Flexible Triggers

Speech can be triggered through clicks, tile entry, scene events and other gameplay interactions.

Scene-Friendly Controls

The interface is designed to feel approachable, readable and useful during live sessions.
