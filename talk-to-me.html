Foundry VTT Module

Talk To Me

A module for ambient NPC dialogue, chatter bubbles and reactive conversations inside Foundry VTT.

Ambient Speech

NPCs can speak through on-screen bubbles instead of cluttering the chat log.

Roll Table Support

Dialogue can be drawn from roll tables, keeping repeated interactions varied.

Flexible Triggers

Speech can be triggered through clicks, tile entry, scene events and other interactions.

Versatile Tile Controls

Tiles for Ambient and Global Lighting, Traps, Spells, Movement, Teleports and Tile Resets
