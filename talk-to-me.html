Talk To Me

A module for ambient NPC dialogue, chatter bubbles and reactive conversations inside Foundry VTT.

Ambient Speech

NPCs speak through on-screen bubbles instead of cluttering chat.

Roll Table Support

Dialogue can be drawn from roll tables for varied interactions.

Flexible Triggers

Speech can trigger from clicks, tiles and scene events.

Versatile Tile Controls

Controls for lighting, traps, spells, movement, teleports and tile resets.
